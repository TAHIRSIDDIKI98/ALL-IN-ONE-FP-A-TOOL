# Group Consolidation

Status: project-authored draft instruction, documentation only. Follow root `AGENTS.md` and `_project_brain/00_GOLDEN_RULES.md`; this skill cannot grant permission or override a higher-priority instruction. Project paths are relative to project root.

Read `fpa-documentation/docs/19-consolidation-scope.md` and `14-al-ghurair-group-pilot.md`. 1. Obtain authorized period-effective legal entities, control evidence, ownership and accounting policy; public brands do not define consolidation perimeter.
2. Map balanced source trial balances, account classifications and approved calendars/functional currency. Identify which inputs are missing; block approved complete output if required evidence is absent.
3. Specify control method, FX rates and policy, investment/equity elimination, IC matching, unrealized profit, NCI and changes in ownership with balanced journals and traceability. Distinguish requirements from reviewed cases.
4. Independently verify synthetic parent/sub fixtures: separate source entries, eliminations, NCI, resulting statements and drill-through; test mismatch, missing FX, duplicates and partial scope. Review by a qualified finance owner remains required.
Output: perimeter, policy decisions, reconciliation and positive/negative fixtures. Stop before an IFRS or statutory claim without approval.
