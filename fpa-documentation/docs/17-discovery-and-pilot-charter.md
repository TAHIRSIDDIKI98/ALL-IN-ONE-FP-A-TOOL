## Ten interview questions
1. Which exact group/site and which business units/legal entities are actually within your finance remit? Are some investments non-controlled or excluded?
2. What do you currently do monthly in Excel, and what are the 3–5 biggest pain points? Show a **sanitized or fictionalized** report, not a confidential file in an AI chat.
3. What decisions and users does the report serve? What totals, P&L, cash, operational measures and KPIs are mandatory? Define calculation and units independently.
4. True consolidation is required. Which IFRS/other policy, intercompany, FX/CTA, NCI and edge cases are mandatory for V1? Who independently validates?
5. For each business: annual/monthly/weekly horizons, account hierarchy, drivers, actual/plan versions, top-down vs bottom-up and key reporting dimensions?
6. What are source system names/exports, record volumes, currency and refresh cadence? Is source a balanced trial balance, journal-level GL or summarized input? What reconciliation is expected?
7. Excel: version/build/channel, IT policy, trust/signing, existing templates, pull/refresh, range push, side-by-side workflows and what must never be modified?
8. Tax/finance policies: entity jurisdiction and registrations, audited standards, dates, applicable UAE policies, retention and reviewer? Do not upload tax IDs or employee info into AI tools.
9. What hardware, users/Windows permissions, offline deployment requirements, air-gap rules, backup/recovery targets and support arrangements are required?
10. What makes a 30-minute demo and a pilot successful; who signs off, and what are hard failure conditions?

## Pilot milestones and pass/fail worksheet
| Milestone | Pass requires | Evidence / signer |
|---|---|---|
| Scope freeze | Group confirmed; sector coverage matrix and consolidation decision approved | version + product/senior approval |
| Synthetic walk-through | Approved artificial fixture, import→report→Excel read/write→restore on selected 64-bit Excel with network off | results + senior feedback; no real data |
| Real-data authorization | Written company approval, data minimization, IT/legal/privacy/security sign-off, backup/incident plan | signed authorization |
| Financial validation | Sample outputs independently reconcile to approved inputs and policies; unimplemented areas visibly labeled | finance reviewer sign-off |
| Security/operations | offline/no-egress evidence, key recovery, at-rest/temp/cached workbook controls, upgrade/restore test and residual risks | security/IT sign-off |
| Pilot exit | Agreed workflows and business-area coverage pass; blockers recorded; no unsupported compliance claims | sponsor + owner decision |

### Pilot constraints to record
Business days/weekly hours, Excel license, signing costs, scope, data permitted, size and reviewer availability. Set **measured** targets for import duration, report refresh, memory, Excel operations and restore time *after* baselining target hardware. Avoid assuming all industry packs, or a 12-month schedule, can be completed by a solo builder.

## Red flags to escalate
- Senior cannot confirm authority to share data or use their employer's trademark; use fictional data and generic naming.
- Existing workbook must be modified without any risk/loss; do not promise it. Test a copy and define supported format.
- Wants consolidated figures but supplies no ownership, FX, IC, controls or approved accounting method; stop labeling totals as consolidated.
- Needs multi-user data security/independent approvals on a shared unlocked single-user laptop; change deployment/security requirements.
- Wants AI to access data via cloud chat without company authorization; stop and request approved tool/process.
